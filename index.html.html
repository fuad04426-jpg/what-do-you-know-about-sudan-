Sudan

Sudan is a country in northeastern Africa. It is bordered by Egypt to the north, the Red Sea to the northeast, Eritrea and Ethiopia to the east, South Sudan to the south, the Central African Republic to the southwest, Chad to the west, and Libya to the northwest. The capital of Sudan is Khartoum. The country has a diverse culture and history, with influences from Arab, African, and Islamic traditions. Sudan has faced various challenges, including political instability and economic difficulties.
